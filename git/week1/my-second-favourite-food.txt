Masala Dosa - South Indian Dish

Ingredients : instant Mixed batter , 1 tea spoon oil, garlic, onion,  carrot, smashed Potatos , Green chillis, salt ,coriander

Instructions

On a heated Pan keep oil,then take a little amount of mixed batter in a small tiny bowl and pour on it in a circle form
keep it om high flame and then add smashed potato,garlic,chilles,onion ,carrot,salt n coriander on it.
Toss on high heat for 2–3 min until evrything is cooked and looks crispy.
Serve hot!
