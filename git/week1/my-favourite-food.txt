Butter Chicken  — Quick Recipe

Ingredients: Butter Chicken 250g, yogurt ½ cup, onion, 2–3 tomatoes, ginger-garlic paste, oil,butter, chilli powder, turmeric, coriander powder, garam masala, kasuri methi, cream, salt.

Instructions:

Mix Chicken + yogurt + spices + lemon juice. Marinate 20 min.
Pan-fry Chicken , onion & until slightly charred. Remove.
Cook onion + ginger-garlic, then add tomatoes + spices. Cook until soft; blend if desired.
Add water and simmer 5–7 min.
Add cream, kasuri methi and paneer. Simmer 2–3 min.
Garnish with coriander.

Serve with naan or rice.
